We’re thrilled to be part of Feel the Sound, an exhibition exploring how we experience sound—not just with our ears, but with our entire bodies and across space. After the opening at the Barbican centre, the exhibition now lands in Tokyo, at MoN Takanawa: the Museum of Narratives. Running from November 20 2026 to March 22 2027, the show brings together a diverse collection of sound-based installations that are immersive and playful. Through works by artists and creators from Japan and around the world, visitors are invited to explore the relationship between sound and their own bodies.

On this occasion, we’re proud to be exhibiting two of our own instruments: Chowndolo and Stacco. Both instruments have been extensively used in performance and research contexts, but adapting them for public exhibition required rethinking several aspects—materials, interface layout, and sonic feedback. It was a chance to reimagine our work not only as tools for inquiry but as artworks capable of withstanding and thriving in the wild.
