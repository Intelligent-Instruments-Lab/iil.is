Federico Visi and Thor Magnusson co-edited a special issue of the Organised Sound journal on embedded algorithms. The issue seeks to investigate how algorithms are embedded in the instruments, tools and technologies of contemporary music and sound art practices. How are algorithms, such as those of machine learning or artificial life, applied as part of new musical instruments, compositional processes and sound installations? This enquiry is very different from current uses of machine learning in music production, in services such as Suno or Udio; it is rather an investigation into how musicians and sound artists are doing what they have always done: exploring how new and emerging technologies can be applied as musical tools.

Check the special issue [here](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/organised-sound/issue/F3BA5CAABDB0F3B4FF25B94E3A19C56E)
